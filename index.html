Code-mixed intent & date extraction · Razorpay AI Buildathon 2026

Dobaara

Recovers failed UPI AutoPay and eNACH debits. Its NLP core reads what customers reply, in English, Hindi or Hinglish, and turns it into an intent and a promised payment date.

0.932
macro-F1, held-out
93.3%

7
intent classes
  • Keyword / regex lexicon written before any data existed, so it couldn't overfit to the test set; LLM parser must beat it to ship.
  • Date normalisation of phrases like "5 tarik ko", "kal tak", "mahine ke end mein" into calendar dates.
  • Gold labels as rules (dom:5, +1, eom) so human judgement is separate from calendar arithmetic.
  • Parser error in the loop: the simulator injects the measured confusion matrix, so mistakes cost money in the results.
"Treat the reply scores as a smoke test." The 180 replies were written by me, so I report that limit up front.

Multilingual speech + entity extraction · Paytm Build for India AI Hackathon 2026

MoneyTrace

An autonomous emergency teammate for UPI fraud victims. It listens to a victim's report in their own language, extracts the financial entities that matter, and links them into a graph that exposes mule-account rings.

  • Speech-to-text with Sarvam AI for Hindi, Tamil, Kannada and English voice reports.
  • Information extraction with Gemini: transaction IDs, UPI handles, amounts, red flags and a severity call, packaged as sealed evidence.
  • Entity triples → knowledge graph with Cognee (add → cognify → search) to connect scammer infrastructure across victims.
  • Calm, step-by-step guidance generated for the victim in real time over server-sent events.

Barrier classification in the patient's language · Health-a-thon 2026

Setu

An assistive follow-up agent that helps cancer care teams find patients who dropped out of treatment, and understand why from what the patient or caregiver says.

  • Closed-label text classification into 8 non-clinical barriers: money, paperwork, travel, lodging, family, felt better, fear, unaware.
  • English, Hindi and Hinglish keyword rules with word-boundary control (dar$ ≠ dard) and Devanagari substring matching.
  • Safety first: an emergency-card check always runs, with reviewed exclusions ("blood test" is not bleeding). Ambiguous matches fail safe.
  • Never clinical: symptom words are logged and routed to a nurse, never answered by the model.
